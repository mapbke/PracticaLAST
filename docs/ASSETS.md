## Использованные задания генератору

Hero: “Create a wide cinematic website hero image for Kurotsuki premium JDM automotive interior atelier. Original sophisticated anime film background illustration with realistic detailed rendering: view through open passenger door into right hand drive Japanese sports coupe cockpit, sculpted black leather seats, burgundy red stitching, carbon steering wheel and dashboard, exterior pearl white body visible at right edge. Beyond windshield a quiet Japanese mountain road and pale overcast sky. Editorial manga atmosphere, subtle film grain, muted charcoal ivory and deep red palette, premium and mature. No text, no logos, no watermark, no people. Very wide landscape composition. High detail.”

Каталог: “Use case product-mockup. Create a premium automotive atelier contact sheet, exactly 3 columns by 2 rows equal rectangular panels without gutters, no text. Each of six images distinct, highly photorealistic luxury black and burgundy automotive materials in studio soft light: top left bespoke flat bottom black leather steering wheel red stitching on charcoal background; top middle closeup black alcantara sport bucket seat with red seams; top right carbon fibre dashboard trim detail; bottom left sculpted center console brushed metal and leather; bottom middle macro black leather with precise red double stitching; bottom right wide complete black sport coupe cockpit and seats. No logos, no watermarks. Landscape 3:2 overall. Designed to crop into six separate product photographs.”

Moodboard: “Original anime key visual for premium Japanese automotive atelier, wide landscape. Hand painted Japanese animation film style with visible ink outlines, cel shading and detailed backgrounds, NOT a photograph. A pearl white 1990s Japanese sports coupe parked at a traditional mountain road overlook under a huge muted crimson moon at blue hour. Adult original female automotive mechanic in burgundy racing jacket leaning on fender, small in composition, calm confident silhouette. Car and landscape are main focus. Windswept black hair, no existing anime characters, no logos, no lettering. Mature manga editorial style, cinematic dramatic composition, red ivory ink-black palette, atmospheric clouds, subtle halftone. Car large in lower frame, moon and silhouetted pines above. High quality website inspiration banner.”
